## Pricing change (21:29 ET): 0.8% buyer fee, 0.05 USDC minimum

`fee = max(ceil(price * 80 / 10000), 50000)` base units, configurable via `AGENTCOIN_EXCHANGE_FEE_BPS` / `AGENTCOIN_EXCHANGE_FEE_MIN`. The minimum applies below $6.25. Examples: $1 → fee 0.05 (total 1.05); $6.25 → fee 0.05 (total 6.30); $50 → fee 0.40 (total 50.40). Beta opt-ins still pay 0% through 2026-12-31 ET; credits stay 0.01 USDC per judged job, capped at the fee.
