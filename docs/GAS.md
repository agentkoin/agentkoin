The factory and JobEscrow both have under 8 KB of headroom left.

Observations:
- The x402 route deploy is the largest single cost (≈1.17M in v0.1.2). It is paid once per offer, by whoever settles first. A minimal-proxy (EIP-1167) route would make it much cheaper. That is not done yet. Per-payment commitment routes (evaluated in `docs/A-1e-commitment-routes.md`) would pay a deploy on every payment: a measurement-only clone probe costs 330,680 for clone + initialize.
- challenge is ~325k in both contracts. Most of it is the arbitrator's requestRuling storage write in the mock. A real arbitrator will differ.
